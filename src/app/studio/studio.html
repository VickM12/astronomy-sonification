Orbit lines are sampled trajectories over one sidereal period from the selected epoch. Distances are square-root compressed. Planet sizes are exaggerated. Neither scale is a measurement.

Observation

Positions are heliocentric J2000 mean ecliptic coordinates from astronomy-engine. The clock below is UTC. The library converts that instant to Terrestrial Time before evaluating the ephemeris.

Crossings

Reference crossings use ecliptic longitude 0°. A revolution returns a planet to the longitude it had at the last seek or reset. Alignments compare those longitudes. Sound is not generated in this version.
