Orbit lines are sampled trajectories over one sidereal period from the selected epoch. Distances are square-root compressed. Planet sizes are exaggerated. Galilean moons are magnified around Jupiter so they clear Jupiter's disc. None of those scales is a measurement.

Observation

Planet positions are heliocentric J2000 mean ecliptic coordinates from astronomy-engine. Moon distances in the planet list are measured from Jupiter. The clock below is UTC. The library converts that instant to Terrestrial Time before evaluating the ephemeris.

Crossings

Reference crossings use ecliptic longitude 0°. A revolution returns a planet to the longitude it had at the last seek or reset. Alignments compare those longitudes. A sounding pulse is placed on the audio clock at the fraction of the step where the crossing fell.
