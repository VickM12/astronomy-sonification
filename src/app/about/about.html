Harmonia Mundi

An instrument made from orbits

This observatory shows the eight planets from Mercury through Neptune. Their places come from Astronomy Engine’s VSOP87 state vectors, rotated from the J2000 mean equator into the J2000 mean ecliptic. Distance and speed are the lengths of those vectors. Nothing in the view is a circular stand-in for a calculated position.

The time you set is UTC. Astronomy Engine then converts that instant to Terrestrial Time for the ephemeris. Sidereal periods, used later for orbital frequencies, are the NASA NSSDCA values, not the tropical periods printed in the summary fact sheet.

Playback speed is simulated days per real-time second. It is separate from any future musical tempo. Seeking rebuilds the longitude anchors, so a jump does not fire the crossings it skipped, and the next step can detect those crossings again.

Scientific pitch, musical scales, synthesis, and export are later milestones. They will read this same simulation. They will not replace it with a prettier orbit.
