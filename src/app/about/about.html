Harmonia Mundi

An instrument made from orbits

This observatory shows the eight planets from Mercury through Neptune, and Jupiter’s moons Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. Planetary places come from Astronomy Engine’s VSOP87 state vectors. The moons come from JupiterMoons, measured from Jupiter’s center. Both are rotated from the J2000 mean equator into the J2000 mean ecliptic. Distance and speed are the lengths of those vectors. Nothing in the view is a circular stand-in for a calculated position. Moon distances on screen are magnified so the moons clear Jupiter’s disc.

The time you set is UTC. Astronomy Engine then converts that instant to Terrestrial Time for the ephemeris. Sidereal periods, used for orbital frequencies, are the NASA NSSDCA values, not the tropical periods printed in the summary fact sheet.

Playback speed is simulated days per real-time second. Tempo chooses how long a crossing rings. The audio-seconds-per-day control spaces those notes on the audio clock. The three are separate. Seeking rebuilds the longitude anchors, so a jump does not fire the crossings it skipped, and the next step can detect those crossings again.

Press Play to hear the orbits. Scientific mode maps each sidereal frequency into sound, either by one shared scale factor or by folding each planet into a chosen octave. The four moons share one other factor, so Ganymede sits at the low edge of the register and their period ratios stay intact. Musical mode starts from those mappings and can snap the pitches to a scale. Next alignment jumps to the next planet–planet conjunction or opposition. The formulas and the resulting hertz stay in the observation panel. A spectral image and a PDF score can be downloaded from that panel. WAV and MIDI export are still ahead.

The orchestral instruments (strings, cello, flute, bassoon, clarinet, French horn, trumpet, harp, piano) play recordings of those instruments, pitched to each planet’s frequency. The recordings are from tonejs-instruments by N. P. Brosowsky, licensed CC BY 3.0, and are fetched from a public CDN the first time an instrument is chosen. Sine, warm pad, bell, pluck, and deep bass are synthesized here.
